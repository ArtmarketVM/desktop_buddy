What goes

  • The "Privacy and local data" essay and its six bullets: one Read row opens it instead.
  • The "Local data controls" disclosure: its three controls become rows.
  • Save retention, Save preferences and both "I understand" checkboxes: changes save on the spot; clearing asks inline, the way deleting a goal does.
  • The "Tracking details" link and the status helper sentence: the status row says it in four words.
  • The permission paragraph with Check permission / Reset and ask again: when access is missing the status row turns into a lime callout with one Allow access action; reset shows only if allowing fails.
  • The "No screenshots are taken" line: it moves into the one-line description under the tracking switch.

What stays, reworded

  • Track goals and suggest completed work switch, with a single line under it.
  • Status: lime dot "Running · receiving activity", grey dot "Paused", violet dot "Needs access".
  • Working days as pixel chips; Working hours as two time fields on one line.
  • Keep history select: Until I delete it, 7, 30, 90 days.
  • Preview of the AI context (opens the local snapshot below the card).
  • Clear… turns the row into "Clear activity and finished goals? · Clear permanently · Keep".
