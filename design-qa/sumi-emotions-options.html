sumi emotions on the grid

Everything is cells on the 7px grid, and every motion is a frame change (steps), never an ease. Three directions: the face alone, pixel marks around the body, or the body itself moving.

Option A

Face only

No marks outside the body. Each emotion is a different eye and mouth bitmap; motion is a two-frame flip (eyes glance, mouth opens, a blink every few seconds). Calmest and the most readable at 32px.

Option B

Pixel marks

What the current set does, redrawn in cells: stepped sound brackets, a climbing trail of squares, a pixel Z, square confetti, blocky arms. Marks are drawn in text colour so they work on both themes; the body stays still.

Option C

Body moves

The body itself carries the emotion in whole-cell jumps: it leans to listen, bobs while thinking, hops when done, flattens to rest, grows a row to stretch. Few extra pixels, the most "alive" of the three.
