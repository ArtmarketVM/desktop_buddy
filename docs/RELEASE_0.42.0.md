# Desktop Buddy 0.42.0

## Daily review and calmer desktop controls

- Keep a durable daily activity review and assign recorded work to the right goals.
- Keep the mascot stationary while chat and reminder cards open in their own transparent window, within the current display's work area.
- Show background reminders without taking keyboard focus. Short reminder text and actions fit without a scrollbar; longer details open in chat.
- Enter clock times with digits only: 0930 becomes 09:30. The colon stays in place in goal deadlines, quiet hours and the daily summary schedule. Support selection, paste, deletion and undo while rejecting invalid hours or minutes.
- Keep deadline calendars inside the visible window, including goals at the bottom of a long Today list. Date, time, duration and Done remain reachable on short screens and when the window is resized.
- Continue delivering reminders through the companion, without native notification banners or notification-permission requests.

Available for Windows x64 and Apple Silicon macOS 14.5 or later.
