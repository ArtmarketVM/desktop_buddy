# Desktop Buddy 0.43.0

## Goal-aware screen observation

- Assess observable progress across open goals using their descriptions and steps, foreground interface text, permitted images and recent context.
- Independently verify proposed results before opt-in automatic completion. Preserve result history, duplicate protection and Undo, including for a goal outside the current focus.
- Propose newly detected goals and steps through the mascot; adding them still requires your acceptance.
- Keep permitted observation separate from Quiet-mode presentation and passive media playback. On macOS, scope the audio activity signal to the foreground process.
- Ground chat explanations in actual observation settings and operational status.
- Honor the configured vision model and keep screenshot controls, privacy exclusions, locked-session checks and bounded observation resources.

A matching title, elapsed time or a deadline is not a completed outcome. Hidden, offline and ambiguous results still require clarification or confirmation.

Available for Windows x64 and Apple Silicon macOS 14.5 or later.
