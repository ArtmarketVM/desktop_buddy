# Mascot message policy

Implemented from the owner-approved launch, summary and quiet-schedule rules on 21 October 2026. Base: `aeb1dd4cad5ce9d6a2171756ad61f983a3e80dff` (main, v0.40.0). This change is not a published release.

## Delivery and settings

- There are no native macOS or Windows system notifications. The Tauri notification plugin and its delivery call have been removed. Old saved notification preferences remain readable and cannot enable OS delivery.
- Working-hours and working-weekday controls have been removed. Their saved fields remain readable for compatibility but have no notification or planning effect. Outside quiet time, deadline, stretch, unlisted-work, matching and completion messages follow activity and evidence rather than a working-hours cutoff. Missed deadline phases collapse into the current phase rather than replaying a burst.
- **Plan Today follows first launch/open each local calendar day**, including background launch after computer startup. Reopening on the same day does not automatically repeat a delivered prompt. **Not now** explicitly requests one planning reminder 30 minutes after that click; ignoring either bubble schedules nothing. Each further explicit Not now can request another single reminder, but there is no automatic loop. Returning after sleep can arm a new day; staying awake through midnight alone does not send a new planning bubble. Explicit quiet time defers planning until a permitted moment. Daily delivery dates are stored separately from the bounded generic message history, so a busy day cannot repeat a daily routine. The pending planning timestamp is saved in `companion_memory`, survives restarting the app and is consumed when its bubble is delivered. Old-date reminders expire; an explicit 30-minute snooze crossing midnight belongs to the new date and cannot duplicate that day’s launch prompt. The selected daily summary takes priority if it and a planning follow-up become due together.
- **Quiet hours** are opt-in, apply every day and can cross midnight. **Quiet days** are selected weekdays that stay quiet for the entire local calendar day. Both use the same resting/no-bubbles rule as manual Quiet mode. Old inactive quiet-hour fields are not silently enabled; existing profiles default to no scheduled quiet days and quiet hours disabled.
- The day summary defaults to **18:00 local time** and has a time input in Settings → General → Nudges (`companion_preferences.summary_minute`). It runs once per local day on the first tick at that time, with five minutes of tolerance for scheduling/resume. It does not depend on an active goal, tracking permissions, provider credentials or Personal Memory. The app must be running. A summary whose due time falls in scheduled quiet time is skipped; manual Quiet mode at delivery also marks it skipped, so it does not arrive later as a backlog. Meeting/away suppression, an open mascot chat, explicit tracking/companion pause and the single fullscreen setting still apply. A pending report expires outside the five-minute delivery window; it is not an evening backlog.
- **Quiet mode is absolute for the mascot:** it keeps the resting pose, closes existing floating chat/cards, suppresses every proactive bubble/toast and overrides completion celebrations. Turning it off does not restore dismissed cards or queue missed phases. Goal activity and unlisted-work time continue locally under existing privacy settings; quiet mode does not pause tracking. The stretch clock starts a fresh interval after quiet mode. Explicit chat is available in the main workspace, without opening a mascot bubble. The sidebar shows “Scheduled” when the quiet schedule is active; clicking it opens General settings to edit the schedule rather than pretending the manual switch can override it.
- Daily planning/summary, deadlines, screen observation and stretches retain their independent switches. Screen opt-in, redaction, evidence verification and disclosure resource budgets are unchanged.
- Old sign-in delays, implicit quiet-hour defaults, 60-minute spacing, three-per-day caps, adaptive frequency penalties and the separate 64-notice presentation cap do not gate these messages. Evidence thresholds, provider request cooldowns and duplicate prevention remain; these are not notification quotas.
- One runtime queue owns all mascot cards, including medium-confidence goal matching (the separate matching toast delivery is removed). Priority is: **deadline now → completion/result questions and notices → daily summary → upcoming/overdue deadlines → activity/new-step/goal-match questions → stretch → planning**. Within an eligible priority, the oldest pending message wins. Launch planning remains eligible even when another card takes priority; it is not lost just because the slot is occupied.
- Higher-priority cards can interrupt lower-priority cards; a displaced card is retained only while still valid. Already-shown planning is an exception: it is not resurfaced after interruption or suppression; only an explicit Not now requests another reminder. Opening a mascot chat suspends card delivery until it closes. Meeting/away/fullscreen suppression defers cards rather than treating suppression as rejection. Quiet mode clears current and queued cards instead of keeping a backlog. The queue is transient across restart; deadline phase history and daily planning/reminder dates remain durable.
- Before every presentation or resumption, the queue rechecks expiry, goal/focus/plan revision, current deadline phase, relevant consent and feature settings, and the source offer for unlisted work or matching. Deleted/rescheduled goals and changed consent discard stale proposals. Deadline wording and the daily report are refreshed from current local data before display. Delivery reservation and deduplication are saved atomically; a storage failure keeps a pending message retryable.
- Duplicate identities/questions coalesce rather than growing a queue of the same message. This does not extend their original lifetime. There is no general inter-card cooldown or daily quota. Activity questions wait for five seconds without input for at most ten seconds after queueing, then become eligible even if input continues. Stretches, completions and scheduled reminders do not wait for a typing pause.
- Meetings use the existing foreground call indicators, not a calendar or proof of background call membership. No bubbles interrupt a detected call. **Meeting time still counts toward the stretch interval, even when the call has no keyboard input; it does not reset the clock.** Media playback is not a notification blocker and does not reset the stretch interval.
- Excluded apps remain excluded from screen observation, browser-address collection, stored activity and AI disclosure. Their exclusion does not block unrelated reminders over that app. The stretch clock can use aggregate input-idle timing without retaining excluded-app content.
- **Hide sumi in full-screen apps** appears once in Settings → General. `companion_preferences.hide_fullscreen` is the only fullscreen presentation switch, including an open mascot chat. Legacy fullscreen/meeting/input-pause fields remain readable but are not delivery controls. Fullscreen does not itself revoke observation consent.
- There is no separate global snooze command, setting, one-hour dismissal or snooze state. Old `buddy_snooze` data is ignored. Manual Quiet mode and its optional quiet hours/days are the only global silence controls. Per-message Not now remains specific to planning or the matching/activity question; it never silences other message types.

## Branches, actions and mascot poses

| Branch | Trigger and effect | Main actions | Pose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Plan Today | First launch/open of the local day, including startup, even with an active goal; deferred during quiet time. Add open goals whose deadline falls on the current local date to Today, without changing focus or completing anything. | Plan today / Not now (remind in 30 minutes) | Neutral |
| Work outside goals | Existing opted-in detector identifies a sustained activity outside the goal list. Keep measured time and completion evidence rules. | Add to Today (or Add completed when verified) / Skip | Questioning |
| Unclear goal | Existing medium-confidence matching result while actively working, including evenings and weekends outside quiet time; delivered through the shared mascot queue. Yes follows the existing guarded focus path; No remembers the rejected tuple; Not now retains its suggestion snooze. | Yes / No / Not now | Questioning |
| Stretch | Actual active work reaches the **selected Stretch interval** in Settings. The interval defaults to 45 minutes for older profiles. Idle outside a detected meeting, sleep, collection gaps, pauses and Quiet mode do not count as continuous work. Detected meetings and media playback do not reset the timer. A clock-time boundary alone does not reset this interval. Delivery restarts the clock, so another reminder requires another selected interval of active work. | I’ll stretch / Not now | Existing stretching pose |
| New step | A concrete screen-observed request belongs to an existing goal and is not already a step. Observation alone never mutates the plan. | Add step / Skip | Questioning |
| Possible completion | Existing verified evidence suggests a particular step or goal is finished; automatic completion is off or not justified. | Mark complete / Not yet | Questioning, then happy after confirmation |
| Verified completion | Existing independent verification and automatic-completion consent allow the result to be applied. | Got it / Undo | Happy/done |
| Deadline before | First eligible tick in the hour before a stored deadline. One identity per goal, deadline and phase. | I’m on it / Reschedule | Neutral |
| Deadline now | First eligible tick at the stored deadline, while still unfinished. | I’m on it / Reschedule | Neutral |
| Deadline after | First eligible tick at least one hour after the deadline, if still unfinished. No endless repeats: per-deadline phase delivery persists in SQLite independently of the general-card history buffer. | I’m on it / Reschedule | Neutral |
| Daily summary | At the selected summary time (18:00 by default), outside quiet hours/days and subject to the delivery rules above. It is a snapshot, not a stop-tracking action: subsequent work still appears in that calendar day’s progress, without another automatic summary. Use actual completed steps and measured goal activity, including accepted unplanned work. Empty history is unknown, not evidence the user did nothing. | Review day / Plan tomorrow | Happy/done, even if some goals remain open |

Waiting to plan is never a global pause: recognised activity, goal matching, deadlines, stretches and completion messages remain independently eligible under their existing settings, privacy and evidence rules. Postponing planning does not enable Quiet mode or pause tracking.

Every message also offers secondary **Chat**, seeded with the situation. Chat never changes focus or completes a goal. Specific proactive help, midday difficulty, automatic distraction/focus warnings and automatic research offers are retired; user-requested chat, analysis and research remain available.

"I’m on it" only acknowledges the reminder; it neither completes the goal nor moves its deadline. Reschedule opens a local date/time field; Cancel makes no request, Save deadline validates a future time and replaces the stored deadline. Reminder identities include the deadline, so old prompts become invalid immediately. A delayed reminder at least a minute past the deadline says it was due at the actual local time and is still open, rather than saying it is due now. Completion and deletion cancel further reminders by making the goal ineligible.

Plan today opens the previous-goals picker even if Today already contains due goals. Selecting additional goals keeps it open until Done. Existing Today goals stay included. Plan tomorrow opens selection for the next calendar day, even if it is a quiet day (quiet settings mute messages, not manual planning), and saves into that date, rather than deferring or mutating the current goal.

## Wording

Follow [sumi tone of voice](SUMI_TONE_OF_VOICE.md). Describe the user's action in the active voice, name the actual activity and avoid technical bookkeeping formulas. Duration is factual; it does not prove completion or sending. The shared coaching guide includes the same rule.

## Action animation signals (0.42.1)

- Committed goal and step completions advance a durable `mascot_completion_revision` in the same SQLite transaction as the event. Manual and AI completion paths share this signal. Repeated completion, rollback, startup and restoring deleted history do not replay celebrations.
- Each chat surface emits `buddy://chat-pose` with its source (`workspace` or `panel`). Listening, thinking and success reach the floating mascot; an idle event from one surface does not clear another surface's active work. Background agent inference exposes a separate thinking flag.
- Quiet mode retains its resting override and consumes suppressed completion signals. Stretching still requires the movement switch and an eligible reminder; both historical movement preference records now save atomically.
- Profile/model text saves after 350 ms or on blur. Other applicable preferences save when committed, without a global Save preferences button. Failed writes keep the draft and expose Retry.

## Validation and remaining gaps

Validation commands/results are recorded in `SESSION_HANDOFF.md`. Local checks cover scheduling, preferences, deadline responses and staleness, restarts/deduplication, privacy boundaries, button behavior, poses and picker selection. Native Windows/macOS installer validation and canonical Windows visual comparisons remain separate from local tests. No installer, app restart or publication is implied by these source changes.
