# Desktop Buddy 0.42.1

## Reliable desktop actions and automatic settings

- Restore the workspace when clicking the macOS Dock icon after closing or minimizing its window, including while the floating mascot remains visible.
- Save settings automatically: apply switches and intervals immediately, save completed clock edits without a separate Save preferences button, and save profile/model text after a short pause or on leaving the field. Show saving status and an explicit retry if storage fails.
- Keep both historical stretch-reminder settings synchronized in one transaction.
- Celebrate committed goal and step completions from manual and AI actions. Avoid replaying old achievements at startup, during Quiet mode, after refresh or when restoring a deleted goal.
- Show listening and thinking from the main Chats page as well as the floating mini-chat. Show thinking during background agent reasoning and return to the normal pose afterward.

Available for Windows x64 and Apple Silicon macOS 14.5 or later.
