# Desktop Buddy 0.41.0

## Mascot messages that follow your day

- Plan Today when you first open sumi each day, including startup. Open goals due today are included automatically. Choose several previous goals and finish with Done.
- Planning's Not now requests one reminder in 30 minutes. Ignoring a planning bubble does not schedule another reminder, and planning never pauses activity, deadlines, stretches or completion messages.
- Set an independent daily summary time in Settings → General → Nudges; the default is 18:00 local time. Work afterwards continues counting toward the day.
- Replace working-hours controls with optional quiet hours and quiet weekdays. Quiet mode always keeps the mascot resting and suppresses proactive bubbles while permitted local tracking continues.
- Use one message queue with explicit priorities, duplicate prevention and checks for stale goals, deadlines and consent. Goal-matching questions now use this queue rather than a separate toast.
- See deadline reminders one hour before, at, and one hour after the deadline. Acknowledge with I'm on it, reschedule with an explicit saved date/time, or chat. Late messages name the actual deadline time.
- Use your selected stretch interval. Detected meetings count toward it without resetting the timer; the bubble waits until the meeting ends. Music no longer suppresses reminders.
- Allow unrelated reminders over excluded apps without observing or disclosing those apps. Use the single Hide sumi in full-screen apps switch in General to control fullscreen presentation.
- Remove system notifications, the separate one-hour snooze and retired unsolicited help/research/distraction cards. Every proactive card offers secondary Chat; its main buttons answer the actual question.
- Use neutral, questioning, stretching and happy mascot poses according to the situation. Copy describes the user's concrete activity in the active voice.

## Delivery and privacy

Quiet mode clears queued cards instead of saving a backlog. Ordinary cards wait during a detected foreground call, while away or while mascot chat is open. Activity questions wait briefly for a pause in input, with a ten-second maximum; stretches and completions do not wait for typing. Pending cards expire and are revalidated before display. The summary requires the app running near its scheduled time, with a five-minute delivery window.

Screen-observation consent, sensitive-app exclusions, redaction, provider credentials and updater signing trust are preserved. Screenshot use and automatic completion remain separate consents. Model judgments can be wrong; confirmation and Undo remain available.

Supported installers: Windows x64 and Apple Silicon macOS 14.5+. Internal macOS signing is not Apple notarization. Automated checks do not establish real-device permissions, microphone behaviour or successful updates on remote computers.
